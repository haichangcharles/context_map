# Findings

## Review Scope
- Assess appendix as a standalone supplementary-material submission.
- Focus on organization, narrative independence, and whether sections clearly support the main paper.

## Key Findings
- Current supplement compiles successfully to an 8-page PDF.
- The document is heavily dominated by prompt documentation; most pages are agent prompt dumps, with little method/analysis support material.
- As a standalone supplement, the front matter is too abrupt: it starts directly with `\appendix` and a short conceptual paragraph, without a reader guide or explicit statement of what the supplement contains.
- The current appendix depends on the main paper for orientation; it repeatedly references sections and concepts from the main paper without giving a compact self-contained recap.
- Participant profile material is useful, but it appears late and feels disconnected from the rest of the supplement because there is no dedicated study appendix section around it.
- LaTeX warnings are minor but present: ACM keywords/CCS warnings, a code-font italic substitution warning, and an overfull participant-profile table.
